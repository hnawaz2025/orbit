Orbit API

The backend for Orbit, a conference companion that answers “who should I actually meet here?” rather than “what’s on at 2pm?”

This is the API. There is nothing to see here; the app is elsewhere.

  • GET /health — liveness, including a real database round trip
  • GET /events — conferences with a loaded corpus
  • POST /ask — a stated problem in, ranked recommendations out
